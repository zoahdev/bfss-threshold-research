# Review findings: shear Ward identity and singlet coercivity

Author: Yicheng Pan. Research checkpoint: 6 October 2026.

**Conditional/provisional. No independent human expert review has been supplied.** These findings summarize the existing analytical material and finite checks; they are not a new mathematical certification. The large-rank BFSS upper bound remains open.

## Conditional identities and scope

The nonlinear Ward identity retains an unsigned weighted stress. For monotone f, the term Df′D is positive, but the shear of the kinetic, potential and fermionic terms has no required upper sign. Scalar supercharge and Clifford dressings reproduce this same family rather than a new rank-suppressed restoring term.

The unbounded linear shear identities in the note require their stated finite-moment and commutator-limit hypotheses. Under the weaker form-domain approach developed in the later [rank-certificate note](../rank-certificate/rank_certificate_audit.md), m₃≤2⟨T⟩/N⁴ is sufficient; an exact equality need not be assumed. Neither formulation supplies a variance upper bound.

## Trial-state obstruction

The note constructs physical SU(2) gauge- and Spin(9)-singlet annular trial packets with energy O(R^(−2)), weighted quadrupole tail at least cR², and quadrupole second moment of order R⁴. Their local oscillator construction, symmetry, tube cutoffs and remainder estimates are part of the stated analytical argument. These are trial states, not zero modes. Consequently they obstruct sector-wide energy-only coercivity but do not contradict a bound in an exact normalized BFSS zero mode.

Under a finite-second-moment zero-mode assumption, adding a small annular packet gives norm-convergent, small-energy states whose weighted-tail observable need not converge. This explains why small BPS residual and a bounded second moment cannot substitute for uniform integrability.

The anisotropic source instability also prevents using ordinary isolated-eigenvalue perturbation theory at zero regulator. Positive-mass compactness, under uniqueness and a finite second moment, is a fixed-rank statement; a rank-uniform regulated susceptibility product remains an independent unproved input.

The construction cites [Fröhlich–Graf–Hasler–Hoppe–Yau](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9904182), [Lin–Yin](https://arxiv.org/html/1402.0055), and [de Wit–Lüscher–Nicolai](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(89)90214-9). The cited all-order wavefunction result is a formal-series statement; it is not silently promoted to an actual zero-mode asymptotic theorem. The note uses the leading local oscillator and a cutoff remainder argument for trial packets.
