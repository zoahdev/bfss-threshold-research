Named-symbol distinctions

D0 in Appendix D prose → D_0
D lines 61–64 define D_0=-F^{-1}C^*p_F; D167,196,201 use D0 for this same local coefficient.

D0 in Appendix H → \mathrm{D0}
H28 explicitly defines D0=+F^{-1}C^*p_F. It is NOT identified with Appendix D D_0, which has the opposite sign.

D1, D2 in Appendix D prose → D_1, D_2
D63–72 give the local coefficients; D119,179,195–201 refer to them without underscores.

phi2, rho0 → \phi_2, \rho_0

W2 → W_2

V3, V4 → V_3, V_4
D92–98 explicitly define the cubic and quartic fast potentials; H74 and I114–123 use compact aliases.

s0 → s_0
H140 defines s0=-F^{-1}S_f; I63 gives the same s_0 definition.

E0,total → E_{0,\mathrm{total}}
H190 identifies the frozen total ground-energy term already written E_0,total in D84–86.

sigma0 / σ0, chi0 / χ0, Usigma / Uσ → \sigma_0, \chi_0, U_\sigma
H200 defines a positive cutoff width, a cutoff equal to one on a smaller tube, and its normalized vacuum. These are named cutoff quantities, not multiplication by zero or by sigma.
