Review boundary
This remains an unreviewed candidate analytical proof. Typesetting checks, finite regression programs, and symbolic identities do not independently establish the full operator estimates. In particular, Appendix D §§4.1–5 still calls for independent reconstruction of every contraction in W(A) and its gap-ratio-uniform perturbation bound. The included finite checks test inverse-Gauss and density Taylor coefficients; they do not verify the entire operator cancellation. No false bound has been demonstrated by this audit, and no full proof certification is claimed.
